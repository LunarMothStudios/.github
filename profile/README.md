## What We Build

Lunar Moth Studios builds software foundations for the cannabis industry. Our work is shaped by the reality that cannabis is not just a product category. It touches patients, operators, communities, compliance, inventory, margins, and trust.

We create systems for cannabis teams that need clearer reporting, stronger workflows, safer automation, and better operational visibility.

## Our Focus

| Area | What We Deliver |
| --- | --- |
| **AI infrastructure** | Managed agentic workflows with approvals, guardrails, and audit visibility. |
| **Dispensary operations** | Tools for reporting rhythm, inventory pressure, task ownership, and operational bottlenecks. |
| **Cannabis bookkeeping** | Automation for categorization, reconciliation, review, and human approval loops. |
| **Patient-centered products** | Software that reduces guesswork and helps people understand outcomes with more clarity. |
| **Responsible systems** | Cannabis software designed for this industry, not retrofitted from somewhere else. |

## Built For Cannabis Pressure

Cannabis operators do not need novelty demos. They need dependable systems that can survive real store traffic, compliance expectations, thin margins, messy reporting, and the weekly rhythm of running the business.

Our infrastructure is designed around:

- approval gates before sensitive actions
- clear ownership for every workflow
- audit-friendly records and change visibility
- integrations across Slack, accounting, inventory, and reporting tools
- practical AI agents that support judgment instead of replacing it

## Products And Systems

### Lunar Guide

A cannabis companion focused on session tracking, reflection, and clearer personal insight. It is built around the belief that cannabis outcomes matter, batch variation is real, and people deserve better than trial-and-error.

### Managed AI Operator

A hands-on engagement for cannabis operators that installs supervised AI systems inside live business workflows. The goal is fewer dropped tasks, clearer reporting, and useful automation that remains accountable.

### Custom Bookkeeping Agents

Tailored financial workflows for cannabis businesses, including expense categorization, reconciliation support, approval controls, and audit visibility.

## Why This Matters

Cannabis deserves software with both structure and soul. The industry can be expressive, creative, and deeply human while still being responsible, measurable, and operationally strong.

That is the work Lunar Moth Studios is here to do.
