## We Build With Curiosity

Lunar Moth Studios is a small creative technology studio making playful, useful, and atmospheric software. We care about tools that feel good to use, games with personality, and digital experiences that carry a little bit of wonder without getting in the way of the work.

Our projects usually live somewhere between:

- **indie games** with expressive systems and memorable worlds
- **creative apps** that help people make, organize, or imagine things
- **web experiments** with polished interfaces and a strong sense of place
- **AI-assisted tools** that feel collaborative, practical, and human

## What Guides Us

| Principle | What It Means |
| --- | --- |
| **Atmosphere matters** | Interfaces should have mood, rhythm, and identity. |
| **Usefulness comes first** | A beautiful tool still has to earn its place in someone's day. |
| **Small details compound** | Motion, copy, timing, layout, and defaults all shape trust. |
| **Build in public when we can** | Shared work invites better ideas and better collaborators. |
